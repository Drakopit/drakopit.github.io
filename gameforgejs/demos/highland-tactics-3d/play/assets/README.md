Place project-owned art, audio, and models here. Highland terrain and landmarks are authored in `scenes/`.

`kaykit-weapons/` contains the unmodified sword and staff glTF models, buffers,
and shared texture from KayKit: Fantasy Weapons Bits 1.0 by Kay Lousberg.
The supplied pack's CC0 license is included in that directory. The hero displays
the sword or staff according to the equipped weapon; game stats remain unchanged.
