Highland Tactics 3D

This project is being developed toward a multi-map JRPG and tactical sandbox: explore connected zones freely, then shift into a dedicated turn-based battle view when combat begins.

Development direction

A world with room to explore

The planned experience pairs an exploration mode with tactical combat and a clean, modular interface.

World

Connected maps

Build multiple zones with portals and free exploration between them.

Combat

Tactical view

Transition into turn-based combat on encounters, then return to exploration.

Interface

Compact HUD

Keep status and actions close at hand with panels that can be toggled.

Characters

Creation & growth

Choose an archetype, shape a character, and progress through XP and levels.

Equipment

Inventory & gear

Manage items and equipment slots with stat bonuses.

World economy

Mobs & shops

Encounter different enemies across zones and trade items or gear in a shop.
